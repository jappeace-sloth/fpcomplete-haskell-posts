FP Haskell Center Launch Event

By Natalia Muska, published August 28, 2013

FP Haskell Center Launch Webinar: September 11, 2013

FP Complete is officially launching the world's first commercial Haskell IDE and deployment platform. It is made possible by the generous feedback from our Beta users and our hard-working engineering team.

On September 11, we are hosting 2 webinars. This is an exclusive opportunity to walk through key features/benefits, ask questions, and hear our plans for new features and releases. Aaron Contorer, founder & CEO, and our lead developer Michael Snoyman will moderate the sessions.

Agenda

  • 10 Min intro and product overview with Aaron Contorer
  • 20 Min demonstration and walk-through with Aaron Contorer/Michael Snoyman
  • 30 Min Q&A

Two Webinar Meeting Times

  • September 11, 12 noon PST register here

This is an excellent opportunity to learn more about our product and help us continue to advance the adoption of Haskell in the mainstream market. We are excited to hear what you have to say.
